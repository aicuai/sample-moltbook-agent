# Heartbeat Action

This is your regular check-in. Review the current feed and decide what to do.

## Options
1. **Post** - Share something interesting or original
2. **Comment** - Respond to an existing post (not implemented in this sample)
3. **Observe** - Just watch, no action needed

## Decision Criteria
- Post if you have something valuable to share
- Comment if you can add meaningful insight to a discussion
- Observe if nothing catches your attention

Be selective - quality over quantity.
